Questions

Frequently asked

Short answers to the ones we get most.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. They are cash prices verified in September 2026 from the manufacturer or a discount platform, and they change. Every price in the app shows its source and the date it was checked, so you can see how current it is.

Does my insurance cover this?

That depends on your plan and, for Medicare, on whether the specific drug is covered. Some drugs are covered for weight management and some are diabetes-indication only. Clicksy shows the coverage rules; your plan makes the decision.

What is dose splitting?

It is using a lower dose than the labelled top dose, drawn from a single approved pen or vial. Clicksy calculates the clicks and milligrams for you. It is not compounded medication and not a research chemical. Whether it is right for you is a clinical decision.

Is my data private?

Your health data is stored on your device and is not uploaded to us. See the privacy policy for the specifics.

Who is this for?

Patients managing their own medication and cost, and clinicians who want coverage and prior-authorization tools without mixing in their own records. The two modes are separate by design.

Clicksy™ — a tracking and cost tool. Not a medical device and not medical advice.

Clicksy does not prescribe, does not change your medication, and does not replace your clinician. Always discuss dose or medication changes with your prescriber.
