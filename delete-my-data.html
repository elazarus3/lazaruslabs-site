Delete your data

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version. You can clear your health records from this installation in Clicksy settings. Android backups and files you exported or shared may remain.

Email us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record associated with your use of Clicksy. Google Play manages billing and keeps its own purchase records.

Step 1. Clear Clicksy data from your device

If you also want us to delete your RevenueCat record, first open Settings → Data & Backup → Show Purchase Support ID and copy the ID if that option is available.

  1. Open Clicksy™.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete My Data, then confirm Delete Everything.
  4. Clicksy removes the records it stores on this device and returns you to the start of the app.

Uninstalling also removes the app's local data. That data includes:

  • Health and body data — weight, height, waist, body composition, activity, sleep, nutrition and dose logs you entered
  • Your medication details — the drug, dose, pen and click settings used to calculate a split regimen
  • Goals and preferences — such as your protein target, units and theme
  • Generated letters and coverage documents, including any prescriber details and letterhead you added

Clicksy does not send us a copy of this data. Manage Android backup copies in your device or Google account settings. Delete exported files and shared documents where you saved them.

Step 2. Request deletion of your RevenueCat record

RevenueCat uses a random app identifier to check Pro access. Its record may include country information, purchase history and entitlement status. It contains no health records.

  1. Email support@lazaruslabs.app with the subject line Delete my Clicksy data.
  2. Include the support ID. If you cannot find it and made a purchase, include the Google Play transaction number from your receipt. Keep health records and payment card details out of your email.
  3. We will confirm by email once the record has been deleted.

Deleting the RevenueCat record does not cancel Google Play billing or remove Google's purchase records. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play under Subscriptions. Opening Clicksy for a Pro access check or restoring a purchase can create a RevenueCat record again.

Transaction and tax records we are legally required to keep Google Play, RevenueCat, and any financial records Lazarus Labs retains Each organization handles its own transaction and accounting records under applicable retention requirements. These are financial records, not health records.

How long it takes

  • Clicksy clears local app data when you confirm Delete Everything.
  • We handle RevenueCat deletion requests within 30 days and email you when we complete the request.
  • Google Play's own transaction records: kept by Google under its own policies and timelines, which are outside our control.

Provider and clinician data

Provider tools generate letters and coverage documents on the device from case details entered for that work. We do not receive or store those details on a Lazarus Labs server. Clinicians are responsible for handling any information they enter and any files they save or share under their own privacy practices and relationship with the patient.
