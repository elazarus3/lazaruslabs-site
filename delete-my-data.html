Delete your data

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version. Almost everything Clicksy stores is on your own phone and nowhere else — no account, no server holding your records, no analytics watching what you do. That means you can delete it yourself, instantly, without asking us.

The only record we can help delete is your subscription record. That one is held by Google Play and by our subscription provider, RevenueCat. Email us and we will remove it.

Step 1 — Delete everything the app has stored

Do this on your phone. It takes a few seconds and it is permanent.

  1. Open Clicksy™.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all data, then confirm Delete Everything.
  4. Clicksy clears your device storage and returns you to the start of the app.

Uninstalling the app deletes the same information. Either route removes:

  • Health and body data — weight, height, waist, body composition, activity, sleep, nutrition and dose logs you entered
  • Your medication details — the drug, dose, pen and click settings used to calculate a split regimen
  • Goals and preferences — such as your protein target, units and theme
  • Generated letters and coverage documents, including any prescriber details and letterhead you added

We hold no copy of any of this. Because your health data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf — and nothing for us to lose.

Step 2 — Request deletion of your subscription records

If you have bought Clicksy Pro or a one-time purchase, a small record of that purchase exists with our subscription provider, RevenueCat, on our behalf. It holds your anonymous app identifier and the fact of your purchase. It contains no health information.

  1. Email support@lazaruslabs.app with the subject line Delete my Clicksy data.
  2. Tell us which subscription record to remove. If you have more than one device or install, include the app identifier shown in your purchase receipt.
  3. We will confirm by email once the record has been deleted.

Please note: deleting your subscription record permanently ends your Pro access and removes your purchase history from our systems. If you change your mind afterwards you can start a new subscription — the old one will not be restored.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Medication details, goals, preferences, generated letters Your device only Deleted by you immediately, using Delete all data in Settings or by uninstalling. We never receive a copy.
Subscription record (anonymous app identifier and purchase history) RevenueCat, on our behalf Deleted within 30 days of your request by email. After deletion you no longer have Pro access.
Payment card details Google Play We never see or store your card details. Google handles the payment and keeps its own transaction records under its own policies, which we cannot delete for you.
Transaction and tax records we are legally required to keep RevenueCat and our accounting provider Retained for the period required by law. These are financial records, not health records, and contain no health information.

How long it takes

  • On-device data: immediate — it is gone the moment you tap Delete Everything.
  • Subscription record: within 30 days of your request. We will email you when it is done.
  • Google Play's own transaction records: kept by Google under its own policies and timelines, which are outside our control.

Provider and clinician data

Clicksy keeps patient mode and provider mode separate. Letters and coverage documents are generated on the device from the information entered for that session, and are not linked to an account or stored on a server by us. If a clinician uses the provider tools, their handling of the information they enter is governed by their own privacy practices and their relationship with the patient.
