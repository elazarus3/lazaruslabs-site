Clicksy™

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version. Clicksy stores your health information in app storage on your device and does not send your health records to Lazarus Labs. We do not operate an account service or server that stores those records. The app has no advertising or activity-tracking SDK.

Android may back up or transfer app data depending on your device settings. Clicksy uses RevenueCat to check Pro access. Google Play and RevenueCat process purchase and restore information without your health records. See Purchases and subscriptions.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Clicksy mobile application and this website. It does not cover a clinic that uses Clicksy provider tools — their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy practices and your relationship with them.

What the app stores

Clicksy keeps the following in the app's storage on your device:

  • Health and body data — weight, height, waist, body composition, activity, sleep, nutrition, and dose logs you enter.
  • Your medication details — the drug, dose, pen and click information needed to calculate a split regimen.
  • Goals and preferences — such as your protein target, units, and theme.

This information is written to your device's local app storage. It is not transmitted to us. We do not have a copy of it, and we cannot see it.

Depending on your Android settings, the operating system may back up or transfer Clicksy data and restore it on this or another device. If you export or share a file from Clicksy, the copy is kept wherever you chose to save or send it.

What we do not do

Specifically, Clicksy does not:

  • create accounts or require you to sign in;
  • send your health records to Lazarus Labs;
  • include third-party advertising or activity-tracking SDKs;
  • sell, rent, or share your health information with anyone;
  • use your health data for machine learning or model training.

Provider mode

Clicksy separates patient mode from provider mode. A clinician using the provider tools works without any of your patient record, and without your own data carried over — the two modes do not mix. Letters and coverage documents are generated on the device from the information entered for that session and are not linked to any account or stored on a server by us.

Notifications

If you enable dose reminders, the notification is scheduled on your device. We do not receive notification content, and we are not part of the messaging delivery system.

Deleting your data

To clear data from this installation, choose Delete My Data in Settings and confirm Delete Everything. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Android backups and files you exported or shared may remain. We do not have your health records and cannot delete those copies for you.

Email support@lazaruslabs.app to request deletion of the RevenueCat record we use for Pro access. Google Play manages billing and keeps its own purchase records.

Full step-by-step instructions are on our delete your data page.

Purchases and subscriptions

Clicksy is free to use. A Pro subscription or one-time purchase is optional.

  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your card details.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage Pro access. Clicksy uses a random installation identifier to check Pro access, including for people using the free version. RevenueCat can receive purchase information from Google Play when you buy or restore Pro and when Clicksy checks an existing purchase. This can include the product, purchase date and whether access is active.
  • Country information. RevenueCat uses the transaction country when available. Otherwise it may infer a country from your IP address and discard the address after doing so.
  • What RevenueCat does not receive: your health data, your dose or weight logs, your name, your email address, or anything else you enter in the app.
  • It is transmitted securely. This information is sent over an encrypted connection.
  • How purchase information is used. RevenueCat uses it to manage Pro access, support purchases and report subscription activity on our behalf. We do not sell or rent it.
  • Cancel any time. Manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store under Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not cancel a subscription.

This website

Our website does not include analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond and, when requested, to locate or delete an associated RevenueCat record. Please do not include health records or payment card details in email.

Children

Clicksy is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, can be sent to support@lazaruslabs.app. That is our single contact address for everything relating to Clicksy.

Clicksy™ provides medication tracking and cost estimates. Always discuss dose or medication changes with your prescriber.
