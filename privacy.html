Clicksy™

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version. Clicksy stores your health information on your own device. The app does not upload it to us. There is no account, no server holding your records, and no advertising or activity-tracking SDK watching what you do.

One thing does leave your device: if you buy a subscription, purchase information is handled by Google Play and by our subscription provider, RevenueCat. Your health data is never part of that. See Purchases and subscriptions.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Clicksy mobile application and this website. It does not cover a clinic that uses Clicksy provider tools — their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy practices and your relationship with them.

What the app stores

To do its job, Clicksy keeps the following on your device only:

  • Health and body data — weight, height, waist, body composition, activity, sleep, nutrition, and dose logs you enter.
  • Your medication details — the drug, dose, pen and click information needed to calculate a split regimen.
  • Goals and preferences — such as your protein target, units, and theme.

This information is written to your device's local app storage. It is not transmitted to us. We do not have a copy of it, and we cannot see it.

What we do not do

Specifically, Clicksy does not:

  • create accounts or require you to sign in;
  • upload your health data to a server;
  • include third-party advertising or activity-tracking SDKs;
  • sell, rent, or share your health information with anyone;
  • use your health data for machine learning or model training.

The single exception to the list above is a subscription purchase, described in the next section. It never involves your health data.

Provider mode

Clicksy separates patient mode from provider mode. A clinician using the provider tools works without any of your patient record, and without your own data carried over — the two modes do not mix. Letters and coverage documents are generated on the device from the information entered for that session and are not linked to any account or stored on a server by us.

Notifications

If you enable dose reminders, the notification is scheduled on your device. We do not receive notification content, and we are not part of the messaging delivery system.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything Clicksy has stored by using the app's own reset option, or by uninstalling the application. Because your health data lives on your device, we cannot delete it for you — and we have no copy of it to delete.

Subscription records are the one exception, because they are held by Google and by RevenueCat. Email support@lazaruslabs.app to ask us to delete them, and see the next section for how to cancel.

Purchases and subscriptions

Clicksy is free to use. Some features require a Pro subscription or a one-time purchase, and buying one is entirely optional. If you never make a purchase, no purchase information is collected at all.

  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your card or payment details — Google processes the payment and we only learn whether a subscription is active.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat is our subscription infrastructure provider. It receives what Google tells it about the purchase: which product was bought, when it started, and whether it is currently active. The app also sends RevenueCat an anonymous app user identifier so a subscription can be restored after you reinstall.
  • What RevenueCat does not receive: your health data, your dose or weight logs, your name, your email address, or anything else you enter in the app.
  • It is transmitted securely. This information is sent over an encrypted connection.
  • It is not sold. Purchase information is used to provide and support your subscription. It is not sold or rented to anyone.
  • Cancel any time. Manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store under Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not cancel a subscription.

This website

Our website is a static site. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels. It sets no cookies at all. Any information you choose to send us by email is used only to reply to you.

Children

Clicksy is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your health information is sensitive

Because Clicksy deals with weight, medication and body data, we treat your privacy seriously. Keeping your data on your device is a design decision, not a policy promise — there is no database for us to leak.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, can be sent to support@lazaruslabs.app. That is our single contact address for everything relating to Clicksy.

Clicksy™ is a tracking and cost tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not replace your clinician.
